Each is made from the photographer's full-size original: a wide crop for landscape screens (`-wide-1672`, `-wide-3344`), a portrait crop for phones (`-tall-1000`, `-tall-2000`) and a 480x500 menu tile (`-tile`). All are lightly graded (shadows lifted, saturation down 10%, warmed toward the site's cream, the lowest part of the frame softened) and encoded as WebP at quality 80 (74 for tiles). To add a photo for another scope, keep the same five files and set `backdrop` on the facet in src/lib/facets.ts.
