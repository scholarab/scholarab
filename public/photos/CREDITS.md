The Wikimedia Commons photos added 2026-09-23 carry their own licenses. CC BY and CC BY-SA require the author, license and source to be credited where the photo is used: the home carousel prints a short credit on each card and /credits/ lists every photo in full (the menu tiles have no room for text). The edited crops of a CC BY-SA photo remain under CC BY-SA. CC0 and public domain photos need no credit. The credit facts live in src/lib/photo-credits.ts, which src/lib/photo-credits.test.ts checks against the facets and the files.

Each is made from the photographer's full-size original: a wide crop for landscape screens (`-wide-1672`, `-wide-3344`), a portrait crop for phones (`-tall-1000`, `-tall-2000`) and a 480x500 menu tile (`-tile`). All are lightly graded (shadows lifted, saturation down 10%, warmed toward the site's cream, the lowest part of the frame softened) and encoded as WebP at quality 80 (74 for tiles). The ruled-paper scan is cut into the top 1073 rows (`lined-top`) and an 11-rule band that repeats below it (`lined-band`), both at the scan's native 1326px width, WebP quality 78. To add a photo for another scope, keep the same five files, set `backdrop` on the facet in src/lib/facets.ts, and add its record to src/lib/photo-credits.ts. The 2026-09-23 set was graded by a script with the same four steps (a 0.9 gamma on the dark end, saturation 0.9, red x1.025 and blue x0.965, a blur blended into the bottom quarter) and cropped to 16:10 wide, 10:13 tall and 480x500; six sources narrower than 3344px were upscaled for the `-wide-3344` file.

The eight Programs menu tiles added 2026-09-23 are public domain (U.S. federal government works, or released by the photographer) or CC0, so none needs a credit; /credits/ lists them all the same. Each is a 480x500 crop of the full-size original at its original colour, with no grade, and there are no wide or tall crops because only the menu shows them.
