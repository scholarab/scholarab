# ScholarAB Trademark Policy

The ScholarAB source code is open source under [AGPL-3.0](./LICENSE), and the
data is open under [CC BY-SA 4.0](./DATA-LICENSE.md). **Neither license grants
any right to use the ScholarAB name or brand.**

This is not an exception carved out of open source. AGPL-3.0 section 7(e)
expressly permits declining to grant trademark rights, and essentially every
open source project does exactly this. You can take the code and the data. You
cannot call the result ScholarAB.

## What is claimed

Unregistered (common law) trademark rights in Canada, arising from continuous
use in association with an online scholarship directory service since March
2026:

- The word mark **ScholarAB**
- The ScholarAB logo mark and the `Scholar` + `AB` wordmark lockup
- The domain **scholarab.ca**

## What you may do without asking

- State accurately that your project is built on, forked from, derived from, or
  uses data from ScholarAB. Nominative reference is fine and is encouraged by
  the attribution requirement in the data license.
- Keep the existing copyright and attribution notices in a fork. You are
  required to, in fact.
- Link to scholarab.ca.
- Write about, review, or criticize the project by name.

## Pre-approved: schools, districts, and counsellors

You do **not** need to email anyone first. If you are an Alberta school, school
district, guidance department, counsellor, teacher, library, or non-profit
serving students, permission is granted here to:

- Use the ScholarAB mark, unmodified, to link to https://www.scholarab.ca from
  your website, guidance page, student portal, newsletter, or slide deck.
- Print it on a poster, handout, or bulletin board display.
- Say that your school "uses" or "recommends" ScholarAB.

The asset is at [`/brand/scholarab-mark.svg`](https://www.scholarab.ca/brand/scholarab-mark.svg).

Three conditions, and they are the whole of it:

1. **Don't alter the mark.** No recolouring, stretching, cropping, rotating, or
   adding elements. Scale it freely.
2. **Link it to scholarab.ca**, wherever a link is possible.
3. **Don't use it as your own logo**, or in a way that suggests ScholarAB
   produced, endorsed, or is affiliated with your organization.

This permission is revocable, but in practice it will only ever be withdrawn for
a use that misleads students. If you want to do something adjacent that isn't
listed, ask, and the answer is very likely yes.

## What requires written permission

Everything below is outside the pre-approved use above:

- Naming a fork, deployment, product, app, or service "ScholarAB", or anything
  close enough to be confused with it.
- Using the ScholarAB logo or wordmark as the identity of your own site or app.
- Registering a domain, social handle, or app listing containing "ScholarAB" or
  a confusingly similar variant.
- Implying endorsement by, affiliation with, or official status from ScholarAB.

A public deployment of a fork must be renamed and rebranded. Attribution goes in
the footer and the README, not on the logo.

## Why this matters here

ScholarAB is used by Alberta high school students to decide where to send real
applications against real deadlines. A fork carrying the ScholarAB name that has
stopped being maintained, with deadlines a year out of date, does direct harm to
students who trust the name. The trademark line is what keeps the name attached
to the version that is actually being verified.

## Enforcement

If you're not sure whether a use is okay, ask first: contact.scholarab@gmail.com.
Good-faith uses are generally fine and permission is usually granted quickly.
Unauthorized uses will be asked to rename before anything else happens.
