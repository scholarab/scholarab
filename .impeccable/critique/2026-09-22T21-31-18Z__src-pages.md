## Design specificity verdict
The skin is ScholarAB; the structure is interchangeable. Specific: the lined-notebook "Worth knowing" note, the "built by a high school student from Medicine Hat" line, days-left chips, the quiz's student-voice hints. Generic: dark nav + skyline + giant headline + counting stat row, 4x2 card grid, pill-filter rows, 01/02/03 steps, dark CTA block, 4-column footer, and small uppercase labels above nearly every heading. The headline is a mission statement any competitor could write, over a Calgary skyline for a site built for small-town students. The biggest missed signature: every listing has a lastVerified date and none is shown.

Deterministic scan: source scan clean (0). Rendered scan 164 desktop / 190 mobile warnings across 20 pages: brand mint on dark (78, house palette), wide-tracked uppercase labels (45-65, label style), labels above headings (10, house style, the same habit the design review flags), hero contrast (6, false positive per glyph measurement), program chip truncation (house style), hidden dropdown text (false positive). Two real: /about "How this works" cards collapse to a 44px column on screens 900px and narrower (about.astro:235 still sets `grid-template-columns: 44px 1fr` after the badge was removed), and /saved empty box has no side padding on phones. The live overlay could not run: the site's CSP blocks the injected script and WebAssembly (correct behaviour for production).

## Overall impression
The rows, the quiz mechanics and the notebook voice are good. What undermines the site is what it claims as its moat: trust. Verification is invisible, 80 estimated deadlines are shown as fact, and numbers disagree with each other, sometimes on the same page. The single biggest opportunity is to make verification the visible signature of every listing.

## What's working
1. Directory rows put amount and deadline first: big green amount, days-left chip, due date, Save, Apply. Scans fast on a phone.
2. The quiz: one question per screen, student-voice hints, Previous, survives refresh, and the homepage mini-quiz hands off mid-flow to /match.
3. The notebook note: the one element with an unmistakable voice, telling a student the thing an older friend would.

## Priority issues
1. [P1] Trust signals hidden; estimated dates presented as fact. lastVerified exists on all 1,416 listings and is never rendered. 80 deadlines are last year's date rolled forward, disclosed only inside the handwritten note while the card says "Deadline May 14, 2027 / 234 DAYS LEFT" and hubs list it as OPEN NOW. Fix: "Checked Sep 2026" on every card and row; estimated dates read "Around May 14 (last year's date)" with no day count; decision-critical conditions (nomination required, must already be enrolled) become structured flags in the facts card. Command: /impeccable clarify, /impeccable harden.
2. [P1] Numbers disagree. Homepage shows Medicine Hat 22 in the header dropdown and 20 in the city chips on the same page; Edmonton 63 vs 66, Cochrane 16 vs 27; /deadlines 926 vs its guide card 932; /educators says a 6-question quiz; urgency colour for the same days-left differs across directory, /saved, programs and detail. Fix: one count source and one urgency scale for every surface; facet counts recomputed against active filters. Command: /impeccable harden.
3. [P1] Quiz results do not deliver. "We found 20 scholarships" is a cap (RESULT_LIMIT = 20); results lack deadlines and status; save uses the star glyph; a closed award is recommended with no tag; school step ignores the chosen board; the city step is 24 full-width tiles on a phone. Fix: "Your top 20 of 64" plus a pre-filtered directory link; deadline and status on every result; drawn bookmark icon; board-filtered schools; type-ahead city with the 6 biggest pinned. Command: /impeccable harden, /impeccable distill.
4. [P2] Filters overload phones and differ by page. Scope is 27 chips by count; on a phone the filter panel expands about 1,000px inline and results change out of view; hubs use a sidebar with different facets. Fix: "Where you live" select/type-ahead with Province-wide and National pinned, a phone sheet with a sticky "Show N results", one filter bar everywhere. Command: /impeccable adapt, /impeccable layout.
5. [P2] Dead ends and a broken page. Closed listings end in a grey "Closed" button with no reopen hint or open alternatives; program pages lead with "PROGRAM COST: See provider" in display type; /about cards collapse to one word per line on phones. Fix: "Closed Sep 1. It usually reopens in [month]. Remind me" plus three open alternatives; cost and grades as plain columns; fix the /about grid rule. Command: /impeccable onboard, /impeccable polish.
