# ScholarAB — 2026/2027 School Year Plan

_Prepared July 6, 2026. Grounded in the current dataset: 156 scholarships (30 active after the July 2026 expiry cleanup), 97 research programs (70 with TBA deadlines), regions from Medicine Hat to National._

## Where we are

The 2025/26 cycle is over. Most scholarship deadlines fell between March and June 2026, so the catalog is now largely dormant: 126 scholarships are inactive and **none have an `open_date` set**, which means nothing will reopen automatically for the new cycle. 70 of 97 research programs show "TBA" deadlines. Data was last verified in bulk in March–April 2026. The pipeline now rebuilds the site daily and auto-expires both the JSON and the database, so freshness going forward is automated — but reopening for 2026/27 is manual work.

## Phase 1 — Summer reset (July–August 2026)

The goal is to enter September with a full, verified 2026/27 catalog. Work through all 126 inactive scholarships and update each with its 2026/27 deadline and an `open_date`; once `open_date` is populated, the nightly auto-sync will re-activate each award on the right day with no further effort. Prioritize by traffic value: Alberta-wide (72) and National (24) awards first, then the city collections (Medicine Hat 19, Edmonton 13, Calgary 10, Red Deer 9, Lethbridge 8). Providers typically publish next-cycle dates in August; anything unpublished gets last year's deadline plus one year, flagged `pendingReview` until confirmed. In the same pass, refresh the 70 TBA research programs — most summer-research programs (SHAD, HYRS, WISEST, verto, etc.) open applications between October and February, so capture their fall open dates now.

## Phase 2 — Back-to-school launch (September 2026)

Refresh the homepage for the new cycle: update the "Updated for 2026" copy and metadata to 2026/27, regenerate the sitemap, and re-verify the hero totals. September is when Grade 12 students start searching, so time an Instagram/TikTok push ("what's open right now" + major early deadlines like Loran, Schulich, TD) to the first two weeks of school. Add a "Major national awards" collection page — Loran (due ~October), Schulich, TD Community Leadership have early-fall deadlines that students routinely miss.

## Phase 3 — Peak season operations (October 2026 – May 2027)

Scholarship season peaks March–May, with a secondary national peak October–December. Cadence: the automated daily expiry/rebuild plus the Monday link-checker keep the catalog clean; add a monthly 30-minute manual verification pass over everything closing in the next 60 days (the `lastVerified` field tracks this). Keep the email deadline-alert list growing — it is the retention channel; promote it on the site each time a "Closing this week" carousel is full.

## Phase 4 — Wrap and rollover (June 2027)

Repeat the summer reset, but this time the `open_date` fields from Phase 1 make it incremental: verify next-cycle dates as providers publish them instead of rebuilding from scratch.

## Product improvements for the year

Ranked by impact on students, effort noted:

1. **Grad-year aware matching** (medium effort) — the Match quiz already exists; letting a student say "I'm in Grade 11, class of 2028" and filtering to awards they'll be eligible for next cycle would make the dormant summer catalog useful year-round.
2. **"Opens soon" state on cards** (small) — the `future`/`Opens` status already renders; once `open_date` is populated (Phase 1), dormant awards become browsable as "Opens Sep 15" instead of disappearing, keeping the site full in summer.
3. **Admin: require open_date on deactivation** (small) — the admin panel should prompt for next cycle's open date whenever an award is closed, so the catalog never goes dormant again. Also add a guard against placeholder entries reaching production (the "test" scholarship incident).
4. **Expand beyond Medicine Hat parity** (content, ongoing) — Calgary (10) and Edmonton (13) are underrepresented relative to population versus Medicine Hat (19); target 25+ each by December 2026.
5. **Educator kit refresh** (small) — update the /educators page with a 2026/27 one-pager counsellors can print in September.
